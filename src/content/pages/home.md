**Joe Pavia Jr. has been a trainer/driver of quality standardbred race horses for over 44 years!**

We currently maintain up to 14 horses in our stable and are always looking for new owners to join our stable. Whether you are a first-time investor or someone who is already active in the industry we encourage you to contact us.

We stable in Wind Gap Pennsylvania on a beautiful farm near Pocono Downs, Meadowlands, Harrah's Philadelphia, Yonkers Raceway, and Tioga Downs. In the winter, our stable is at Sunshine Meadows in Delray Beach, Florida. Anyone wishing to race at any of these tracks this summer please give us a call. We also are available to train horses and winter them in Florida and send them back ready and fit to race come spring for outside trainers. Me and my crew will take excellent care of you horses and send them back up north looking great and ready to race.

Please explore our [website](/), or our [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/paviastable) Page for further details.

We are always looking for quality caretakers to work in Pennsylvania and Florida.
