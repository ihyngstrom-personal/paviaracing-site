---
title: "Steelhead Hanover wins at Pompano Park!"
date: 2016-04-11
categories: ["wins"]
---

The Pavia stable had two winners in a row this evening at Pompano and a third winner at Harrah’s Philadelphia.  The second winner on the night at Pompano is Steelhead Hanover who took the lead at the start and defeated a group of open pacers in an impressive 1:51.2.

Congratulations to winning owners: <a>Dijo Racing Inc</a> - FL, <a>Joseph Barbera</a> - NY, <a>Agc Stables Inc</a> - FL, <a>Pint Size Racing LLC</a> - FL
