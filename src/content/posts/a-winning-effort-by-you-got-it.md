**WILKES-BARRE, Pa. (April 14, 2026)** — You Got It took the lead and never gave it back. Sent off at 5-1 in a mile trot for non-winners at Pocono Downs at Mohegan Pennsylvania, the 6-year-old stallion, trained by Joe Pavia Jr. and driven by George Napolitano Jr., won by 3¾ lengths in 1:54.2.

Starting from the outside post 8, he was in front at every call: by 1¼ lengths at the first quarter (27.4), the half (56.4) and the three-quarters (1:25.1), two lengths clear at the stretch call, and 3¾ at the wire. His last quarter was 29.1.

Mrstery Deal rallied from fourth at the three-quarters to finish second in 1:55.1, with What's The Talk third in 1:55.2. The 0.90-1 favorite, Little Town Road, was disqualified after a break. You Got It paid $12.00 to win, and the $2 exacta (8-2) returned $64.20.

You Got It, by Chapter Seven out of Tweet Me, was bred by John Cummins and is owned by Vincent Ferriero Jr., Jeffrey Hess and Pint Size Racing LLC.
