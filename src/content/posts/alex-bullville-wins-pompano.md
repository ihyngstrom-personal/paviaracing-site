---
title: "Alex Bullville wins at Pompano!"
date: 2017-11-14
categories: ["wins"]
---

Alex Bullville brings in the first Pavia Stable winner of the 2017-18 Pompano Park winter meet.  Defeating a field of conditioned pacers on a sloppy track in 1:54.2.  Congratulations to winning owners:  <a>Joe Pavia Jr Stable Inc</a> - PA, <a>John Whitig</a> - PA, <a>Jeffrey Hess</a> - FL, <a>Pint Size Racing LLC</a> - FL.
