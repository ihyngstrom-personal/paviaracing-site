---
title: "Arodasi wins again at Pompano Park!"
date: 2016-12-23
categories: ["wins"]
---

The second winner on Sunday night was the 7 year old mare Arodasi.  Arodasi raced in her best style by coming from off the pace to win by a neck in 1:52.3.  This win propels her into the top Filly and Mare Open to compete against the best mares at Pompano this winter. Congratulations to winning owners: <a>Peter Shank</a> - FL, <a>Agc Stables Inc</a> - FL, <a>Joe Pavia Jr Stable Inc</a> - PA
