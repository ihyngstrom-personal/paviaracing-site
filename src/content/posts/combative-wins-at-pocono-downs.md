**WILKES-BARRE, Pa. (October 31, 2026)** — Combative refused to be beaten at Pocono Downs. Sent off the 2-1 second choice in a $25,000 claiming pace, the 4-year-old gelding, trained by Joe Pavia Jr. and driven by Ridge Warren, rallied from sixth to win by a nose in 1:51.4.

Starting from post 4, he was sixth, 6¾ lengths behind the leader, at the first quarter (26.2). Warren moved him off the rail and he was sixth and outside, 3¾ lengths back, at the half (54.4), then fifth and outside, 2½ lengths back, at the three-quarters (1:22.4). He was second, a half-length off the lead, at the stretch call and got up in the final strides with a last quarter in 29.0.

D A Love Boat, the 1.50-1 favorite, driven by Johnathan Ahle, was second, a nose back, also in 1:51.4. Rjs Red Devil, driven by Gregory Merton, was third in 1:53.0. Combative paid $6.00 to win, and the $2 exacta (4-5) returned $14.60.

Combative, by Captaintreacherous out of Acquavella, was bred by Hanover Shoe Farms Inc. and is owned by Steven Held, Pint Size Racing LLC, John Whitig and Donald Kayser.
