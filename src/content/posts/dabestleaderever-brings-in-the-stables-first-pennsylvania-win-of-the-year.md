---
title: "Dabestleaderever brings in the stables first Pennsylvania win of the year!"
date: 2016-04-11
categories: ["wins"]
---

Third third leg of the Pavia Stable triple play came from Dabestleaderever at Harrah's Philadelphia.  David Miller drove for the Pavia Stable and came from off the pace against a field of conditioned pacers to win in 1:53.1.

Congratulations to winning owners:  <a>John Whitig</a> - PA, <a>Michael Rich</a> - FL, <a>Al Solomon</a> - FL, <a>Lawrence Chimerine</a> - FL
