**WILKES-BARRE, Pa. (June 30, 2020)** — Deli-Delite took command at the half and never looked back. Sent off at 2.50-1 in a $6,800 trot for non-winners of $3,000 in their last five starts at The Downs at Mohegan Sun Pocono, the gelding, trained by Joe Pavia Jr. and driven by Anthony Napolitano, won by 2 lengths in 1:56.2.

Starting from the rail, he was second, 1¼ lengths behind Muay Hanover, at the first quarter (28.4), then took the lead by 1¼ lengths at the half (57.4). He was in front by a length at the three-quarters (1:26.4), 2 lengths clear at the stretch call, and 2 at the wire, with a last quarter in 29.3.

Therapeutic, the 0.40-1 favorite, was second in 1:56.4, with Muay Hanover third in 1:57.0.
