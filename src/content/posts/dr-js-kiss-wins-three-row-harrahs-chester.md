Her last win was as impressive as her first two wins but this time it was a new lifetime mark of 152:1. That lowered her lifetime best by 1 full second and I can see her lowering that mark as the year goes on. Since giving her a rest in February and putting her on Lasix she has not been beaten in 5 races including two qualifiers in Florida. Her toughest test will be in 2 weeks when we will try her against the best 3 year old fillies that Pennsylvania has to offer in the sire stakes at Harrah's .
