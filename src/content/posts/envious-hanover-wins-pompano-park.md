---
title: "Envious Hanover wins at Pompano Park!"
date: 2017-12-27
categories: ["wins"]
---

On a warm 73 degree night in south Florida, Envious Hanover defeated a field of conditioned mares in 1:55 on the front end all the way.  Congratulations to winning owners: <a>Dijo Racing Inc</a> - FL, <a>Pint Size Racing LLC</a> - FL, <a>Jeffrey Nanna</a> - NY, <a>Joe Pavia Jr Stable Inc</a> - PA.
