**WILKES-BARRE, Pa. (June 21, 2026)** — Grand Cayman won this one by the narrowest of margins. The 8-year-old gelding, trained by Joe Pavia Jr. and driven by Matt Kakaley, led all the way in an $11,000 pace at Pocono Downs at Mohegan Pennsylvania and held off Rock The Belles by a nose, both timed in 1:50.4.

Starting from post 2, he left on the outside, shown by the bubble at the first quarter (26.4), and led by a half-length. He was on top by 1¼ lengths at the half (54.4), the three-quarters (1:23.1) and the stretch call, and went on to win by a nose after a last quarter of 27.3.

Rock The Belles, the 1.40-1 favorite, was second, with Hard To Catch third in 1:51.1. Grand Cayman, sent off at 1.70-1, paid $5.40 to win, and the $2 exacta (2-6) returned $14.20.

Grand Cayman, by Always B Miki out of Barbuda Babe, was bred by Frederick Hertrich III and is owned by Vincent Ferriero Jr.
