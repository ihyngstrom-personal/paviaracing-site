**EAST RUTHERFORD, N.J. (July 25, 2026)** — Grand Cayman made a big-league score at the Meadowlands. Sent off the 0.60-1 favorite in a $12,500 pace for horses and geldings, the 8-year-old, trained by Joe Pavia Jr. and driven by Tim Tetrick, took the lead before the half and won by 4 lengths in 1:50.0.

Starting from the rail, he was second, a nose off the lead, on the outside at the first quarter (26.4), then took command by 1¼ lengths at the half (55.0). He was 1¾ lengths ahead at the three-quarters (1:22.4), 2¾ at the stretch call, and 4 at the wire. His last quarter was 27.1.

Seen Here was second and Euphoria N third, both timed in 1:50.4. Grand Cayman paid $3.20 to win, and the $1 exacta (1-7) returned $6.40.

Grand Cayman, by Always B Miki out of Barbuda Babe, was bred by Frederick Hertrich III and is owned by Vincent Ferriero Jr.
