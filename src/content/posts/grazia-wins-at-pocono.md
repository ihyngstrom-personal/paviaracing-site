**WILKES-BARRE, Pa. (April 20, 2026)** — Grazia did what a 1-10 favorite is supposed to do. The 3-year-old filly by Huntsville out of Ramalama, trained by Joe Pavia Jr. and driven by Jason Bartlett, left from the outside post 8, took the lead and won a $12,500 pace at Pocono Downs at Mohegan Pennsylvania by 1½ lengths in 1:54.4.

She was on top at every call, by 1¼ lengths at the first quarter (28.2), 1½ at the half (57.3), 1¾ at the three-quarters (1:26.3), and 2 at the stretch call, before easing to 1½ at the wire. Her last quarter was 28.1.

She's In The Juice, third early, closed to finish second in 1:55.0, with Ok My Way third in 1:55.3. Grazia paid $2.20 to win.

Grazia was bred by Whiskeyontherocks Racing and is owned by Vincent Ferriero Jr., Pint Size Racing LLC, Dominic Pavia and Steven Held.
