---
title: "Hot Mess Hanover breaks her maiden at Pocono!"
date: 2016-07-11
categories: ["wins"]
---

Coming from mid pack with a steady close down the stretch from 4th Hot Mess Hanover, a two year old filly out of Cantab Hall, beat a field of older maiden trotters in 1:59.2. Congratulations to the winning owners: Edmund Smith Estate - NY, David Smith - NY, James Giannuzzi - NY, Daniel Giannuzzi - NY.
