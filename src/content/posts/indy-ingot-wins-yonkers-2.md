date: 2017-07-29

Indy Ingot closed very fast down the stretch from the 3 hole to defeat a field of conditioned pacers at Yonkers in 1:53.4.  Congratulations to winning owners: Dijo Racing Inc - FL, John Whitig - PA, Jack Shaffer - NJ, Jeffrey Nanna - NY
