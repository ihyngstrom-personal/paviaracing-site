**YONKERS, N.Y. (April 16, 2026)** — Jas Bluestone made every pole a winning one. Sent off at 5-1 in a $40,000 claiming allowance trot at Yonkers Raceway, the 7-year-old gelding, trained by Joe Pavia Jr. and driven by Tyler Buter, took command early and held off Eyes Of Justice by three-quarters of a length in 1:55.4.

He was on the front end at every call, by 2 lengths at the first quarter (28.4), 1½ at the half (57.3) and the three-quarters (1:26.2), and three-quarters of a length at the stretch call and the wire. His last quarter was 29.2.

Eyes Of Justice, second all the way, finished second in 1:56.0, with For A Dreamer third in 1:56.2. The 1.12-1 favorite, Chulo, broke at the start and finished fifth. Jas Bluestone paid $12.64 to win, and the $1 exacta (3-1) returned $64.30.

Jas Bluestone, by Anders Bluestone out of Ja's Commando, was bred by Pamela Wagner, raced for owner Kathleen Napolitano, and was claimed out of the race by Adamczyk Racing LLC.
