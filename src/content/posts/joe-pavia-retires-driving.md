---
title: "Joe Pavia Retires from Driving"
date: 2016-09-04
categories: ["news"]
---

<span style="color: #000000; font-family: Calibri;">Joe Pavia Jr. had a driving accident on 6/27/16 in 12th race at Pocono Downs. The totter he was driving was on the front end and made a break just before the 1/4 pole as Joe attempted to take the horse off the course to the inside, as there was a horse outside of him, the horse jumped the pylon and lunged back toward the track.</span><span style="color: #000000; font-family: Calibri;">  </span><span style="color: #000000; font-family: Calibri;">The horse behind his horse managed to miss him but the horse sitting 3rd hooked Joe's outside wheel and catapulting him to the ground injuring his neck, hip and a slight concussion. After X-Rays and an MRI Joe was treated in the emergency room and released.</span><span style="color: #000000; font-family: Calibri;">  </span><span style="color: #000000; font-family: Calibri;">After an examination the next day and test results gone over the doctor made a recommendation that Joe retire from driving due to the multiple concussions sustained in his racing career. Joe has 17 horses in training and he will now concentrate on training a public stable both in Pennsylvania and Florida.</span>
