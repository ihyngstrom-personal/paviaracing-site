**MONTICELLO, N.Y. (July 13, 2026)** — Long Shore was the overwhelming choice, and she delivered. Sent off at 0.20-1 in a $20,000 division of the New York Excelsior Series for 3-year-old pacing fillies at Monticello Raceway, the daughter of American Ideal, trained by Joe Pavia Jr. and driven by Matt Kakaley, took the lead at the three-quarters and won by 3 lengths in 1:55.1.

Starting from post 3, she was third, 4¾ lengths back, at the first quarter (28.2), then moved to the outside, shown by the bubble at the half (57.3), 2¾ lengths from the front. She was in front by a length at the three-quarters (1:26.1) and by 2½ at the stretch call, stretching that to 3 at the wire. Her last quarter was 29.0.

Bellbottom Country was second in 1:55.4, with Arya Blue Chip third in 1:56.2. Long Shore paid $2.40 to win, and the $1 exacta (3-5) returned $6.30.

Long Shore, by American Ideal out of Butchie Beach, was bred by Fair Winds Farm Inc. and is owned by Vincent Ferriero Jr., Dominic Pavia, Eric Prevost and Todd Frocione.
