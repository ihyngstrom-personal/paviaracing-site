---
title: "Regalanthropist wins at Harrah's Philadelphia!"
date: 2017-08-14
categories: ["wins"]
---

Regalanthropist drove to the front for the lead against a field of conditioned claimers.  Then Regalanthropist relinquished the lead for a 2 hole trip setting up for a strong move in the passing lane to win by 2 lengths in 1:54.0. Congratulations to winning owners: John Whitig - PA, Al Solomon - FL, Michael Rich - FL, Lawrence Chimerine - FL.
