**WILKES-BARRE, Pa. (October 27, 2026)** — Royal Enterprise made a stalking trip pay off at Pocono Downs. Sent off the 0.80-1 favorite in a $15,000 claiming trot, the 3-year-old gelding, trained by Joe Pavia Jr. and driven by Matt Kakaley, sat second behind the leader the whole way and got by in the stretch to win by ¾ of a length in 1:55.2.

Starting from post 5, he was second, 1¼ lengths behind Pisces Rising, at the first quarter (27.4), the half (57.1), the three-quarters (1:26.0) and the stretch call. He found another gear late and won with a last quarter in 29.1.

Fashion Cantab, driven by Ridge Warren, was second in 1:55.3, with the front-running Pisces Rising third in 1:55.4. Royal Enterprise paid $3.60 to win, and the $2 exacta (5-2) returned $15.20.

Royal Enterprise, by Enterprise out of Itsgoodtobequeen, was bred by Henry Wieseneck and Jon Erdner and is owned by John Whitig, Michael Rich, Jeffrey Hess and Pint Size Racing LLC.
