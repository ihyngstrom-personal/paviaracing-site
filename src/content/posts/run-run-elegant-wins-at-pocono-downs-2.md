**WILKES-BARRE, Pa. (July 14, 2026)** — A week after his last win, Run Run Elegant did it again. Sent off the 1.40-1 favorite in a $15,000 claiming trot at Pocono Downs at Mohegan Pennsylvania, the 9-year-old gelding, trained by Joe Pavia Jr. and driven by George Napolitano Jr., came first over to win by 2 lengths in 1:54.0.

Starting from the outside post 8, he was fifth at the first quarter (27.0) and the half (56.0), then moved to the outside, shown by the bubble at the three-quarters (1:23.4), where he was fourth and 4¼ lengths back. He was third and 1½ lengths from the lead at the stretch call, and then went by to win. His last quarter was 30.1.

Erin was second in 1:54.2, with Roger Rabbit third in 1:55.3. Run Run Elegant paid $4.80 to win, and the $2 exacta (8-4) returned $20.20. He was claimed out of the race by William A. Hartt.

Run Run Elegant, by Rose Run Hooligan out of Jojo P V, was bred by Marcel Brouillard and was owned by John Whitig, Michael Rich, Jeffrey Hess and Pint Size Racing LLC.
