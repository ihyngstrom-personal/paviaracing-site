**WILKES-BARRE, Pa. (August 1, 2026)** — Sainthood left no doubt this time. Sent off the 1.20-1 favorite in an $11,000 trot for non-winners of $5,500 in their last five starts, the 5-year-old gelding, trained by Joe Pavia Jr. and driven by George Napolitano Jr., came first over from third and won at Pocono Downs at Mohegan Pennsylvania by 4½ lengths in 1:52.4.

Starting from the rail, he was third, 2½ lengths back, at the first quarter (27.4) and the half (56.2). He moved to the outside to take the lead at the three-quarters (1:24.0), shown by the bubble, where he was in front by three-quarters of a length. He was 1½ lengths clear at the stretch call and drew off to the 4½-length margin, with a last quarter in 28.4.

Andover Contessa was second in 1:53.3, with Erin third in 1:54.1. Sainthood paid $4.40 to win, and the $2 exacta (1-5) returned $14.60.

Sainthood, by International Moni out of Truffles, was bred by Winbak Farm and is owned by Bay Pond Racing Stable.
