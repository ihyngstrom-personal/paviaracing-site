**WILKES-BARRE, Pa. (September 28, 2026)** — Sainthood was a good-value winner at Pocono Downs. Sent off at 6.20-1 in a $13,500 trot, the 5-year-old gelding, trained by Joe Pavia Jr. and driven by Anthony Napolitano, took the lead before the half and held it to win by a length in 1:53.1.

Starting from post 3, he was third, 2½ lengths back, at the first quarter (27.1), then in front by 1¼ lengths at the half (55.2). He held that margin at the three-quarters (1:24.0) and the stretch call, and won by a length with a last quarter in 29.1.

Give Me A Yankee was second and Purple Lord third, both in 1:53.2. The 1.30-1 favorite, Scudo Hanover, was disqualified after a break. Sainthood paid $14.40 to win, and the $2 exacta (3-4) returned $137.80.

Sainthood, by International Moni out of Truffles, was bred by Winbak Farm and is owned by Bay Pond Racing Stable.
