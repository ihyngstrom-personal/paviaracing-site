**WILKES-BARRE, Pa. (June 16, 2026)** — Sainthood turned a long shot into a big payday. Sent off at 15.40-1 in an $11,000 trot for non-winners at Pocono Downs at Mohegan Pennsylvania, the 5-year-old gelding, trained by Joe Pavia Jr. and driven by George Napolitano Jr., sat second most of the way and got by Pisces Rising to win by 1½ lengths in 1:53.4.

Starting from post 7, he was second, 1¼ lengths off the pace, at the first quarter (27.4), the half (57.0), the three-quarters (1:25.0) and the stretch call. He took over late and won by 1½, finishing with a last quarter of 28.4.

Pisces Rising, the 2.10-1 second choice, led through the stretch and finished second in 1:54.0, with Pc Free Wheeling third in 1:54.3. The 0.80-1 favorite, Call Option, was disqualified after breaking at the start. Sainthood paid $32.80 to win, and the $2 exacta (7-8) returned $105.00.

Sainthood, by International Moni out of Truffles, was bred by Winbak Farm and is owned by Bay Pond Racing Stable.
