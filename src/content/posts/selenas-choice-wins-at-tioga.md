**NICHOLS, N.Y. (August 14, 2026)** — Selina's Choice closed out her series with a win. Sent off at 0.05-1 in the $12,000 Fillies & Mares Summer Pop Up Series Final at Tioga Downs, the 5-year-old mare, trained by Joe Pavia Jr. and driven by Ridge Warren, went to the front before the half and won by 2½ lengths in 1:54.0.

Starting from post 7, she was fourth on the outside, 3 lengths back, at the first quarter (26.4), shown by the bubble, then took the lead by 1½ lengths at the half (55.2). She kept that margin at the three-quarters (1:24.0), was 3 lengths clear at the stretch call, and won by 2½ with a last quarter of 30.0.

Mimosa On The Key was second in 1:54.2, with Wicked Cruizer third in 1:54.3. Selina's Choice paid $2.10 to win, and the $2 exacta (8-4) returned $6.74.

Selina's Choice, by Courtly Choice out of Selinas Joy, was bred by Winbak Farm and is owned by Bay Pond Racing Stable.
