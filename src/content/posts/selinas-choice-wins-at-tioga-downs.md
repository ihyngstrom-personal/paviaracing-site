**NICHOLS, N.Y. (July 24, 2026)** — Selina's Choice won this one by a nose. Sent off at 3.01-1 in a $7,500 pace for fillies and mares at Tioga Downs, she was second most of the way, then came out and got up in the final strides to beat the 0.91-1 favorite, Celia B Money, in a photo.

Driver Ridge Warren had her second, 1¼ lengths back, at the first quarter (26.4) and the half (56.2), and on the outside at the three-quarters (1:24.1), where she was 1¾ lengths behind Celia B Money. She was still second by a quarter-length at the stretch call before getting up at the wire. Both finished in 1:52.4, and her last quarter was 28.3.

Katie Cotton was third in 1:53.3. Selina's Choice paid $8.02 to win.

Selina's Choice is trained by Joe Pavia Jr.
