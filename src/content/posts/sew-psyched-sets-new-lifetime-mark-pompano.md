---
title: "Sew Psyched Sets New Lifetime Mark at Pompano"
date: 2017-03-09
categories: ["wins"]
---

Sew Psyched won drawing away off a first over trip at Pompano Park.  The 6 year old mare completed the mile in a lifetime best 1:52.2. Congratulations to winning owners: <a>Dijo Racing Inc</a> - FL, <a>Pint Size Racing LLC</a> - FL, <a>Jeffrey Nanna</a> - NY, <a>Joe Pavia Jr Stable Inc</a> - PA
