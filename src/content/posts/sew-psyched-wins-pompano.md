title: "Sew Psyched wins at Pompano!"
date: 2016-12-23
categories: ["wins"]
---

The Pavia stable had a two win night on Wednesday also.  The first winner of the night was the 5 year old mare Sew Psyched who led the mile every step, winning by 4 lengths in 1:53.3.  Congratulations to winning owners:  <a>Dijo Racing Inc</a> - FL, <a>Pint Size Racing LLC</a> - FL, <a>Jeffrey Nanna</a> - NY, <a>Joe Pavia Jr Stable Inc</a> - PA.
