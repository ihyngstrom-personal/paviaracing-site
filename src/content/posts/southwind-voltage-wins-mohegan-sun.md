---
title: "Southwind Voltage wins at Mohegan Sun!"
date: 2016-08-31
categories: ["wins"]
---

Southwind Voltage left well from the start driving to the front, then ceded the lead for a rail trip to the three quarter pole. Making another move through the final turn he drew off by 4 lengths to defeat a field of conditioned trotters.

Congratulations to winning owners: John Whitig - PA, Michael Rich - FL, Al Solomon - FL, Lawrence Chimerine - FL
