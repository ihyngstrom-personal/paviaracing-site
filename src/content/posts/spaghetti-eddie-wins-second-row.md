---
title: "Spaghetti Eddie wins second in a row!"
date: 2016-06-08
categories: ["wins"]
---

Spaghetti Eddie won his second race in a row at Harrah's Philadelphia for owners John, Whitig, Jeffrey Nanna and Joe Pavia Stable Inc.  Spaghetti Eddie was entered in a $10 Claiming event, making a move after the 3/4 pole to win by a head in 1:54.4.  He was also claimed out of this race by Melissa Guido Stables, LLC.
