**WILKES-BARRE, Pa. (July 28, 2026)** — Spray The Bar went gate to wire in the slop. Sent off the 1.90-1 favorite in a $14,500 trot for 3-year-olds at Pocono Downs at Mohegan Pennsylvania, the colt by Bar Hopping, trained by Joe Pavia Jr. and driven by Anthony Napolitano, led at every call and won by a length in 1:56.2 over a sloppy track.

Leaving from the rail, he was on top by 1½ lengths at the first quarter (28.2), 1¾ at the half (58.0), 1½ at the three-quarters (1:27.0), and 1¾ at the stretch call, before winning by a length. His last quarter was 29.2.

Almost Free, who chased him the whole way, was second in 1:56.3, with Tolentine third in 1:58.1. Spray The Bar paid $5.80 to win, and the $2 exacta (1-3) returned $11.00.

Spray The Bar, by Bar Hopping out of Julia's Way, was bred by Winbak Farm and is owned by Bay Pond Racing Stable.
