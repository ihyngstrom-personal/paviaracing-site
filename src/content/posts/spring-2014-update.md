We have raced only a few horses this past winter as planned. We primarily come to Florida to get out of the bad winter months up north and to train our 2 and 3 year olds and freshen up our race horses. Florida gives the horse's a great place to train and stay healthy with out all the obstacles of bad weather. There is a reason why spring training in baseball is in the south and not the north. I believe the same goes for the horse who also is a athlete. Stall rent in Pompano is only $100 per month which is very reasonable for a great facility and race track and sunshine.....

We have not missed a day training with our 2 year olds and they all have been in 2:30 as of 2/18/14. The stable has been very successful with the limited horses it has sent to post this winter. Stats below are from Pompano Park Race Track Nov 10th 2013 thru Feb 17 2014.
