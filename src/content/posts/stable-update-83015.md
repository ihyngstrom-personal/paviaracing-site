<h3>********ARODASI WINS 5 OF LAST 6 STARTS*********</h3>
The hard hitting race mare Arodasi is in top form as she recorded her 5<sup>th</sup> win in her last 6 starts. She won her 2<sup>nd</sup> consecutive race at Tioga Downs in the open mares pace going for $14,000 in 150:4. This pushes her 2015 earnings to $56,605 and her lifetime earnings to $340,016…….

The stable in the last 2 weeks has been racing very well. As of 8/16/15 we have had 27 starts with 7 WINS and $57,547 in earnings. As I have said before racing is like any sport or athlete you have peaks and valleys. No one more than myself wish that it was all peaks and we could win whenever we want to but that is not realistic. Post position, classification and racing luck all factors in the end result. And that is all factors if your horse is healthy and sharp which is the hardest part of racing or any sport keeping your players sharp and healthy!!

<strong> ARODASI</strong>: TALK ABOUT A HORSE BEING SHARP!!! She was a WINNER at Tioga Downs in the open mares pace going for $14,000 on Friday night winning her 2<sup>nd</sup> in a row there and 5 wins in her last 6 starts with her usual off the pace tactics. She recorded her 8<sup>th</sup> win of 2015 with a150:4 mile to seal the deal. We will enter her back in to race at Tioga as she has run out of condition options at the moment since she has $33,380 made in her last 5 starts. It is Tioga Downs last week of racing so we will once again hope to spot her where she fits well…..
