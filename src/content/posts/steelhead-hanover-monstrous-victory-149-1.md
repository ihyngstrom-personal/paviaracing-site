Steelhead Hanover increased his lifetime earnings to $515,170 on Wednesday.  With an impressive win in 1:49.1 at Harrah's Philadelphia.  Tim Tetrick, driving Steelhead, initially went to the lead then sat a very patient trip behind the leader.  Turning for home Tim Tetrick took advantage of the passing lane for what the track announcer called a "Monstrous victory".

Congratulations to owners Dijo Racing Inc, Agc Stables Inc, Joseph Barbera, Pint Size Racing LLC.
