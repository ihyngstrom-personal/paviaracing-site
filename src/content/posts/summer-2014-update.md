<span style="color: black; font-family: 'Arial','sans-serif'; font-size: 10pt;">The 2014 racing season at Pocono Downs has featured great purses and fast and furious competitive racing. Our stable has held it's own and done well this year with the age pacers Steelhead Hanover winning in 148:3 and Bet On The Law winning in 148:4. On the trotting side Keystone Thomas has won in 153:3 and the aged pacing mare Arodasi took back to back lifetime marks in 150:4 and 150:1.</span>

<span style="color: black; font-family: 'Arial','sans-serif'; font-size: 10pt;">We started the year with four two year olds and all of them made the races. The pacing colts were led by the speedy but inconsistent hard luck NY bred<strong><span style="font-family: 'Arial','sans-serif';"> K Ryan Bluechip (Art major-Fool  That I Am).</span></strong> He won his baby race and took a mark of 154:2 and then raced in 8 NYSS races with 2 second place finishes and 3 third place finishes with earnings of $31,558. The home bred  pacing colt <strong><span style="font-family: 'Arial','sans-serif';">Kiss Up (Quick Pulse Mindale-Pucker Up)</span></strong>  took a baby race record of 158:1 and we sold him for $12,000.</span>

<span style="color: black; font-family: 'Arial','sans-serif'; font-size: 10pt;"> </span><span style="color: black; font-family: 'Arial','sans-serif'; font-size: 10pt;">We started with two  2 year old pacing fillies. <strong><span style="font-family: 'Arial','sans-serif';">Icappella Hanover (Somebeachsomewhere-I'm Sassy</span></strong>)  she made 6 starts with 2 wins and 2 second place finishes with earnings of $35,000 so far. She has 2 wins and a 2nd place finish in the PASS Stallian stakes and finished 2nd in the final at The Meadows for a purse of $40,000. </span>

<span style="color: black; font-family: 'Arial','sans-serif'; font-size: 10pt;"> </span><span style="color: black; font-family: 'Arial','sans-serif'; font-size: 10pt;">Our other 2 year old filly <strong><span style="font-family: 'Arial','sans-serif';">Heart Major (Art Major-Ru Girl</span></strong>) may be the fastest of the two fillies but she fell behind in training down due to a small injury and sickness. She has had 2 starts and paced in 154:2 with a last 1/4 in 27:2 in her first lifetime start.</span>

<span style="color: black; font-family: 'Arial','sans-serif'; font-size: 10pt;">We will continue to race at Pocono Downs ,Harrah;s Philadelphia and Yonkers Race track till December 1st. We will take a small contingent of horses to Pompano Park race track in Florida around November 4th that will consist of some race horses and yearlings we purchase at the sales. The remaining race horses will come down on December 1st. </span>

<span style="color: black; font-family: 'Arial','sans-serif'; font-size: 10pt;">If you are training race horse's or breaking yearlings Pompano Park gives your horse the best facility to do this. After you pay good money for your yearlings and race horses you would want them to have every benefit and advantage on there side to reach there potential and in Florida w</span><span style="color: black; font-family: 'Arial','sans-serif'; font-size: 10pt;">ith great weather you get that rather than the bad weather and frozen tracks up north.</span>
