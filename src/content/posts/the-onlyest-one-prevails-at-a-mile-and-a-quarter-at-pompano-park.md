---
title: "The Onlyest One prevails at a mile and a quarter at Pompano Park!"
date: 2018-03-12
categories: ["wins"]
---

The Open II class at Pompano was a big night for The Onlyest One.  This horse now has six wins out of nine starts, with the three other starts second place finishes for a total earnings of $37,500.  Wally Hennessey drove, positioning the horse early in the mile from towards the front of the race, overcoming the 7 hole and a short sprint into the first turn.  Sitting on the rail third turning for it appeared as if he would have traffic issues, but Wally found room on the inside for a quick sprint to the lead in the last hundred yards.  The 1-1/4 race timed in 2:22.3 last half in 55.3.

Congratulations to winning owners: J<a>ohn Whitig</a> - PA, <a>Michael Rich</a> - FL, <a>Lawrence Chimerine</a> - FL
