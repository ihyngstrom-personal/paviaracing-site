**YONKERS, N.Y. (July 20, 2026)** — Vengeance Bluechip came from well back at Yonkers. Sent off the 2.13-1 favorite in a $23,000 pace for non-winners of $15,000 in their last five starts, the gelding, trained by Joe Pavia Jr. and driven by Tyler Buter, rallied from fifth to win by a half-length in 1:52.0.

Starting from post 4, he was fifth, 7 lengths back, at the first quarter (27.1), fifth and 8¼ back at the half (55.1), and fifth, 4 lengths back, at the three-quarters (1:23.0). He was third and 3 lengths from the front at the stretch call, and got up in the final strides with a last quarter of 29.0.

Th Colby was second, also timed in 1:52.0, with Star Casino N third in 1:52.1. Vengeance Bluechip paid $6.26 to win.
