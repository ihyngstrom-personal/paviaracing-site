---
title: "White Mountain Top Wins at Pocono!"
date: 2017-05-21
categories: ["wins"]
---

White Mountain Top used some early speed to take the lead first time past the grandstand and continuing in front for the entire mile to win by two lengths in 1:52.0.  Congratulations to winning owners: <a>John Whitig</a> - PA, <a>Michael Rich</a> - FL, <a>Al Solomon</a> - FL, <a>Lawrence Chimerine</a> - FL.
