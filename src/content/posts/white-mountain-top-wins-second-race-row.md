---
title: "White Mountain Top wins second race in a row!"
date: 2017-11-01
categories: ["wins"]
---

White Mountain Top took advantage of a nice second over trip to overcome a field of $12,500 pacers to win at Harrah's Philadelphia in 1:53.3.  Congratulations to winning owners: <a>John Whitig</a> - PA, <a>Michael Rich</a> - FL, <a>Al Solomon</a> - FL, <a>Lawrence Chimerine</a> - FL
