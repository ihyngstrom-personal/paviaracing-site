It has been a nice warm winter in Florida which is a great place for your horse whether it’s a race horse or yearling to be training or racing. Our stable will head north from our winter base in Pompano Beach Florida on April 1st. Any owners looking to send horses or purchase or claim horses to race at Pocono Downs, The Meadowlands, Yonkers or Harrah’s Philadelphia feel free to contact us. We are always looking for new owners to join our stable. The 2015 racing season at Pocono Downs will featured great purses and fast and furious competitive racing.
