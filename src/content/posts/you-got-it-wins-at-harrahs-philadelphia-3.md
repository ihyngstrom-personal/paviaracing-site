**CHESTER, Pa. (June 18, 2026)** — You Got It was too good at Harrah's Philadelphia. Sent off the 1.30-1 favorite in a $10,000 mile trot for non-winners of $7,500 in their last five starts, the 6-year-old stallion, trained by Joe Pavia Jr. and driven by Andrew McCarthy, left from the rail, sat third, then came first over to win by 1¾ lengths in 1:53.1.

He was third, a length back, at the first quarter (28.0), three lengths back at the half (56.2), and then moved to the outside, shown by the bubble at the three-quarters (1:24.0), two lengths from the front. By the stretch call he was in front by a half-length, and he stretched that to 1¾ at the wire, closing with a last quarter in 29.1.

Just Like Magic was second in 1:53.2, with Muscledover, who made the early lead, third in 1:53.3. The 1.70-1 second choice, Frank The Elder, finished sixth. You Got It paid $4.60 to win, and the $2 exacta (1-2) returned $11.20.

You Got It, by Chapter Seven out of Tweet Me, was bred by John Cummins and is owned by Vincent Ferriero Jr., Jeffrey Hess and Pint Size Racing LLC.
