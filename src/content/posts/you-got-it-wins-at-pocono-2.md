**WILKES-BARRE, Pa. (April 21, 2026)** — A week after his last win, You Got It was back in the winner's circle at Pocono Downs. Sent off the 1.50-1 favorite in an $11,000 mile trot for non-winners, the 6-year-old stallion, trained by Joe Pavia Jr. and driven by George Napolitano Jr., came first over and beat Bank On Me by 1½ lengths in 1:53.1.

He was third at the first quarter (27.0), the half (55.4) and the three-quarters (1:23.2), where the bubble beside his position shows he was on the outside making his move. Hand Dover Dan, who made the early lead, still had a 1¼-length lead at the stretch call, but You Got It rallied to go by late and win by 1½. His last quarter was 29.4.

Bank On Me closed from fourth to finish second in 1:53.2, with One After Nine third in 1:53.3. Hand Dover Dan, who made the front, faded to fourth. You Got It paid $5.00 to win, and the $2 exacta (2-1) returned $44.00.

The win came with a field of six after Jack Vernon and Loranga were scratched. You Got It is owned by Vincent Ferriero Jr., Jeffrey Hess and Pint Size Racing LLC.
