**WILKES-BARRE, Pa. (October 5, 2026)** — You Got It added another one at Pocono Downs. Sent off the 2.60-1 favorite in a $16,000 trot for non-winners of $11,500 in their last five starts, the 6-year-old stallion, trained by Joe Pavia Jr. and driven by George Napolitano Jr., came first over and won by 2 lengths in 1:53.3.

Starting from the rail, he was fourth, 2½ lengths back, at the first quarter (27.2) and fourth on the outside at the half (56.2). He took over by 1¼ lengths at the three-quarters (1:24.4), held that at the stretch call, and drew off to win by 2 with a last quarter in 28.4.

Breezy Hall was second in 1:54.0, with Cassius Hanover third in 1:54.3. Pavia stablemate Sainthood, driven by Anthony Napolitano, finished sixth. You Got It paid $7.20 to win, and the $2 exacta (1-2) returned $36.60.

You Got It, by Chapter Seven out of Tweet Me, was bred by John Cummins and is owned by Vincent Ferriero Jr., Jeffrey Hess and Pint Size Racing LLC.
