**WILKES-BARRE, Pa. (October 26, 2026)** — You Got It came from off the pace at Pocono Downs. Sent off at 3.50-1, the second choice in the wagering, in a $16,000 trot for non-winners of $11,500 in their last five starts, the 6-year-old stallion, trained by Joe Pavia Jr. and driven by George Napolitano Jr., made an outside move and won by 1½ lengths in 1:53.3.

Starting from post 4, he was sixth, 4¾ lengths back, at the first quarter (26.2) and still sixth, 4 lengths back, on the outside at the half (55.3). He moved up to fourth, 2¼ lengths from the front, at the three-quarters (1:23.4), was second, a half-length back, at the stretch call, and got to the front to win with a last quarter in 29.2.

Donato Patriot K, driven by Jason Bartlett, was second in 1:53.4, with In A Tiny Way third in 1:54.2. The 1.60-1 favorite, Breezy Hall, finished sixth, and Black Velvet As and Give Me A Yankee were disqualified after breaking stride. You Got It paid $9.00 to win, and the $2 exacta (4-2) returned $89.40.

You Got It, by Chapter Seven out of Tweet Me, was bred by John Cummins and is owned by Vincent Ferriero Jr., Jeffrey Hess and Pint Size Racing LLC.
