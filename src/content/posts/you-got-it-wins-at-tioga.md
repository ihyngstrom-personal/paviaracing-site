**NICHOLS, N.Y. (July 4, 2026)** — You Got It won a three-horse photo on the Fourth of July. Moving up to an Open II trot at Tioga Downs, the 6-year-old stallion, trained by Joe Pavia Jr. and driven by Braxten Boyd, rallied from fifth to win by a half-length in 1:54.0.

Starting from post 5, he was fifth, 6 lengths back, at the first quarter (27.1) and fifth at the half (57.0). He moved to the outside, shown by the bubble at the three-quarters (1:25.0), where he was fourth and 1½ lengths from the front, was third at the stretch call, and then got up to win. His last quarter was 29.0.

The 1.22-1 favorite, Kinnder Thinktwice, was second, also timed in 1:54.0, with Purple Lord, who made the pace, third. You Got It, sent off at 2.28-1, paid $6.56 to win, and the $2 exacta (6-4) returned $16.20.

You Got It, by Chapter Seven out of Tweet Me, was bred by John Cummins and is owned by Vincent Ferriero Jr., Jeffrey Hess and Pint Size Racing LLC.
